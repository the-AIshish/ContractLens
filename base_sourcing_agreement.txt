MASTER MANUFACTURING & SUPPLY AGREEMENT
Effective Date: 1st November 2026
Parties: Auric Consumer Brands Pvt Ltd (Mumbai, India) ["Buyer"] and Pacific Rim Formulations Ltd (Hong Kong) ["Supplier"]

1. PURCHASE ORDERS & MINIMUM ORDER QUANTITY (MOQ):
Buyer shall issue Purchase Orders specifying batch volumes. Supplier agrees to maintain a Minimum Order Quantity (MOQ) of 2,500 units per formulation run. Total estimated annual consideration: USD 45,000 (~INR 37,50,000).

2. PAYMENT & ADVANCE TRANCHES:
Payment terms shall be structured as: 30% advance deposit upon Purchase Order confirmation; 70% balance payable within thirty (30) days from formal quality inspection clearance at destination port in Nhava Sheva, Mumbai (Net 30 post-inspection).

3. QUALITY AUDIT & DEEMED ACCEPTANCE:
Buyer shall have thirty (30) business days from delivery at destination port to conduct third-party laboratory quality testing. Goods shall be deemed accepted only upon affirmative written sign-off by Buyer. Defective batches shall be replaced at Supplier's sole expense within 15 days.

4. TOOLING & INTELLECTUAL PROPERTY:
All proprietary formulation recipes, custom bottle molds, and tooling funded by Buyer shall remain the exclusive intellectual property of Buyer. Supplier shall not use Buyer's molds for any third party.

5. TERM & TERMINATION:
This Agreement commences on 1st November 2026 and expires on 31st October 2027. Either party may terminate for convenience with sixty (60) days prior written notice. Automatic renewal for successive 1-year terms occurs unless Buyer provides written non-renewal notice at least sixty (60) days prior to expiration (Cutoff: 1st September 2027).

6. GOVERNING LAW:
This Agreement is governed by the laws of India. Courts in Mumbai shall have exclusive jurisdiction.
