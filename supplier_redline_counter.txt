REVISED MASTER MANUFACTURING & SUPPLY AGREEMENT (PACIFIC RIM COUNTER-DRAFT)
Effective Date: 1st November 2026
Parties: Auric Consumer Brands Pvt Ltd ["Buyer"] and Pacific Rim Formulations Ltd ["Supplier"]

1. PURCHASE ORDERS & MINIMUM ORDER QUANTITY (MOQ) (REVISED):
Minimum Order Quantity (MOQ) is increased to 5,000 units per formulation run. All prices quoted in USD subject to quarterly currency fluctuation adjustments exceeding 2%.

2. PAYMENT & ADVANCE TRANCHES (REVISED):
Payment terms: 50% advance deposit upon PO confirmation; 50% balance payable against Bill of Lading (B/L) copy prior to shipment release. No payments tied to destination inspection.

3. QUALITY AUDIT & DEEMED ACCEPTANCE (REVISED):
Buyer shall inspect goods at factory gate or within seven (7) calendar days of arrival at destination port. Failure to provide lab test reports within seven (7) calendar days shall constitute irrevocable deemed acceptance of the entire shipment.

4. TOOLING & INTELLECTUAL PROPERTY (REVISED):
Formulation recipes remain Buyer's IP. However, custom molds and manufacturing tooling shall remain the property of Supplier unless Buyer pays an unamortized tooling buyout fee of USD 12,000.

5. TERM & TERMINATION (REVISED):
Initial Term: 1 Year (expiring 31st October 2027). Auto-renews for 2-year periods. Notice of non-renewal must be delivered in writing at least ninety (90) days prior to expiration.

6. GOVERNING LAW (REVISED):
Governed by Hong Kong Law. All disputes shall be subject to arbitration administered by the Hong Kong International Arbitration Centre (HKIAC).
